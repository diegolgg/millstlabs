\maketitle

\section{Question}
One or two sentences. What we want to know and why it matters for the thesis (one line on that).

\section{Setting}
The population or student, the reference bots involved, the dials that are held fixed (by name, from the
explainer), the model and backend. Three to six lines.

\section{What we measure}
The quantity in words, then as a formula. Say what is random (seeds, deals, the model), what is paired across
cells, and what the unit of replication is. If there is a planted truth, say what it is and how we know it.

\section{Prediction}
Dated. A number or a shape, with the reasoning that produced it (the measured rates it comes from). State what
result would make us abandon the idea.

\section{Design}
Factors and levels; seeds (pilot, then by power from the pilot's spread at the minimum effect we care about);
the null (stub) run; the figure that will be made. Decision rule: the condition under which we will say the
prediction held, fixed here.

\section{Cost}
Calls, wall clock, dollars (hosted price per call $\times$ calls), engine time.

\section{Nearest prior work}
Three works, one line each, and what this design adds. Phrase novelty as ``not found in a search on <date>''.
